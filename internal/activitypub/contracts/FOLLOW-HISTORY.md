# Optional history on accepted Follow

Default off. FOLLOW_HISTORY_LIMIT (0–100, 0 disables) bounds the most recent current public Article instances; FOLLOW_HISTORY_INTERVAL_MS (1000–60000, default2000) spaces successful deliveries. Selection and delivery are oldest first within that bound. No startup scan, bulk existing-follower delivery, private/draft/page import, date rewriting or profile-fetch trigger exists.

A verified new Follow persists its Accept and history intents atomically. The worker sends Accept before historical Create. Original activity/object IDs and publication dates survive; the current public Article snapshot is addressed to the personal inbox without a followers-collection cc. Shared inbox routing is disabled. Destination server means remote actor origin. A unique publication/server/object slot prevents resending completed history to the same server, even for another follower or after refollow. Existing ordinary delivery also retains its per-recipient/activity unique key.

Undo or actor block cancels unsent history. A fresh Follow can resume canceled unsent slots after a successful Accept; exhausted sends remain inspectable. Queued withdrawals cancel history and preserve tombstones. An already issued HTTP request cannot be recalled. Delivery is at least once: a crash after remote acceptance can repeat the same activity ID. Fedify may regenerate a signing proof timestamp, while the persisted semantic payload is immutable. Remote deduplication uses stable IDs. Old public posts may appear on remote timelines; no portable profile-only guarantee exists.

Re-enabling never scans existing followers.
